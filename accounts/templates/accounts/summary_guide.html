Summary Selection Guide

Overview

The Summary Selection panel allows you to visualize the health check votes and trends across teams, departments, sessions, and health cards. The visualization capabilities vary based on your role in the organization.

Using Filters

You can filter the data using the following options:

  • Department: Select a specific department or view all departments (Senior Managers only)
  • Team: Select a specific team within the selected department
  • Session: Select a specific session to analyze votes from that session only
  • Card: Select a specific health card to focus on one aspect
  • Time Period: Select a date range to see how votes have changed over time

Note: The filters available to you depend on your role:

  • Team Leaders: Can only view teams in their department
  • Department Leaders: Can view summaries of all teams in their department and summary-level information about other departments
  • Senior Managers: Have full access to view all departments and teams

Understanding the Visualizations

The summary panel presents two main visualizations:

1. Voting Statistics

This shows the distribution of votes (Green, Amber, Red) based on your selected filters. The bar chart provides a visual representation of the vote counts.

  • Green: Good health, no issues
  • Amber: Some concerns, needs attention
  • Red: Critical issues requiring immediate action

2. Trend Statistics

This shows how team members perceive the trend of the selected card(s) or session(s). The overall trend is calculated based on the majority opinion.

  • Improving: Situation is getting better
  • About the Same: No significant change
  • Getting Worse: Situation is deteriorating

Tips for Effective Analysis

  • Start with a broad view (all teams, all cards) to identify general patterns
  • Drill down into specific teams or cards showing concerning patterns
  • Use the time period filter to track progress over time
  • Compare trends across different teams to identify best practices
  • Look for correlations between different health cards
